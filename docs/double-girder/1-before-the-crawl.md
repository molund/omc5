# 1. Before the crawl: the one-off move at span 42 (and 51, and 30)

The gantry is on span 41 (or 50, or 29) and about to move to 42 (or 51, or 30). Planning only. Nothing is built.
Overview and shared notes: [`README.md`](README.md). Next: [`2-the-crawl.md`](2-the-crawl.md).

The normal cycle runs as usual until Support C has been set down behind the forward support. From there this move
differs. The normal-span rule (C in front of A, then A carried behind the front support, from step 4 on in
[`../animation-steps/README.md`](../animation-steps/README.md)) does not apply on double girders.

## The steps

Written for span 41 going to 42. Span 51 repeats it, with the front support going to the tulip between 55 and 56, and span 30 too (before span 31), where the front support goes to the hammerhead between 32 and 33 (a hammerhead, not a
tulip), centred on the unfinished gantry-built guideway in the drawing, not on the whole hammerhead.

1. The normal cycle runs until Support C has been set down behind the forward support.
2. **The crane move.** The trolley crane picks up the forward support and carries it forward a little. The support then
   disappears while the trolley is still holding it, and reappears further down the line on its new support: the tulip
   between 48 and 49 (55 / 56 for span 51). On site it takes three steps (see below), but the animation shows only this
   first part. The flatbed and the crane that sets it down aren't drawn, and how many flatbeds there are doesn't matter.
3. The trusses slide back and pick up Support B.
4. The front lifting jacks carry Support B to where the forward support used to be. It lands across both the westbound and
   eastbound double girders, in the position the forward support would have taken. B is now the front for the crawl.

This move uses the front lifting jacks and the ballast. It is not part of `L`.

Steps 12 to 16 of the normal sequence (picking up, carrying, swinging and lowering the front support) are skipped, because
Support B does the front support's job.

## The crane move, what is expected

- On site, the support is moved in three steps:
  1. The trolley crane lifts the front support onto a flatbed.
  2. The flatbed carries it to its new support past the double girder section (the destination is below).
  3. A crane lifts it onto that support.
- Only the front support is moved. This is settled from theory, not from observation: it hasn't been seen yet.
- No temporary tower is used.
- The destination is the tulip one support past the first support after the crawl: 48 / 49 for the 43 to 47 crawl,
  55 / 56 for the 52 to 54 crawl. For span 30 it is a hammerhead, between 32 and 33. The same code is used for all three spans: the support is centred on the unfinished gantry-built
  guideway in the drawing and not on the whole hammerhead.
