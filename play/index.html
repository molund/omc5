Langley Surrey SkyTrain Launching gantry movement animation

This animation shows how the Clayton Clipper, one of the four launching gantries building the Surrey Langley SkyTrain's guideway in Surrey, BC, moves itself forward one span at a time. It plays on the Clayton Clipper Tracker map, over an aerial photo of the route, from the span the gantry is on today to Bakerview-166 Street Station.

It's a simplified picture of the Clipper's cycle, drawn from watching it at work, not an engineering model.
