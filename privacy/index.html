Privacy

← Map

Comments and your privacy. Comments on Trackside are moving from GitHub Discussions to my own comment server, at comments.olund.ca. To comment there, you sign in with Google or GitHub. That shares your name and profile picture with the comment server, which shows them next to what you write. The server keeps your comments and votes. It doesn't get your email address or your password. It runs on a Google Cloud machine in Oregon, I'm the only one with access, and nothing on it is sold or shared.

Deleting your data. To have everything removed, open the comments, click your name, choose the option to request deletion, and email the link it gives you to mike@olund.ca. I'll delete your account, comments and votes, usually within a few days. You can also just email me and ask.

Analytics and storage. I use Google Analytics to see how many people visit and which pages they read. It sets cookies in your browser. The map also keeps your layer settings in your browser's session storage, so they survive a reload. That stays on your device, and it's gone when you close the tab.

Who's behind all this is on the About page.
