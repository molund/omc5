Clayton Clipper Tracker: Surrey Langley SkyTrain guideway construction progress

A map of the Surrey Langley SkyTrain guideway in Surrey, BC, following the Clayton Clipper gantry span by span as it builds the elevated guideway along Fraser Highway, alongside the spans lifted in by mobile cranes. It's updated from regular walks and rides along the line.

Trackside has every update, newest first, with photos and comments. The map itself needs JavaScript.
